## 12. Strategic Recommendations for the Target Paper

### 12.1 Framing the “BioInspired” Angle
BIP reviewers expect the “bioinspired” label to mean something concrete. Map the cross-modal disagreement concept to neuroscience: *“The human brain resolves ambiguity by comparing predictions across sensory modalities (Ernst & Banks, 2002; Stein & Stanford, 2008). Our embedding-level disagreement metric operationalizes this multisensory conflict detection for VLM-based diagnosis.”* Even a short paragraph in the Introduction will immunize the paper against “not bioinspired enough” critiques.

### 12.2 Dataset & Ethics Shield
Because the paper relies on public fundus datasets, add a concise ethics statement: *“We use publicly available de-identified datasets (REFUGE, RIM-ONE, GAMMA, DRISHTI-GS) previously collected under IRB-approved protocols. No new patient recruitment was performed.”* This satisfies IEEE PSPB expectations and preempts reviewer skepticism about medical AI ethics.

### 12.3 Preprint Policy
**Do not post the title or abstract on arXiv before submission** (or, if already posted, ensure the metadata is non-identifiable and do not advertise it). The BIP CFP does not explicitly ban preprints, but double-blind anonymity is fragile. After acceptance, update the arXiv version with the IEEE copyright notice and DOI.

### 12.4 Page Budget (6–8 pages)
- **Page 1:** Title, authors (omitted in review), abstract (150–200 words), 4–6 keywords, 1-column introductory figure (fundus image + VLM architecture teaser).
- **Pages 2–3:** Introduction (clinical motivation + bio-inspired hook + contributions) + Related Work (3–4 key citations per sub-topic: glaucoma AI, medical VLM, uncertainty estimation, cross-modal fusion).
- **Pages 4–5:** Methodology (formal definition of cross-modal representation disagreement, lightweight computation, architecture diagram).
- **Page 6:** Experiments (dataset description, metrics: AUC, ECE, Brier score, AUROC of uncertainty signal), results table, 1–2 performance figures.
- **Page 7:** Ablation study (entropy vs. disagreement, calibration curves, failure-case visualizations) + Discussion (limitations, future work).
- **Page 8:** Conclusion + References (aim for 20–30 refs, all in IEEE format).

### 12.5 Reproducibility Artifacts
Create an anonymous GitHub mirror (via anonymous.4open.science) containing:
- Inference scripts for the disagreement metric.
- Pre-trained model weights or links to HuggingFace (if space permits).
- A `README.md` with exact package versions and a 5-minute quick-start.
Mention in the paper: *“Anonymized code and evaluation scripts are available at [anonymous link].”*

### 12.6 Comparable Venue Fallbacks
If BIP desk-rejects or reviewer feedback is too harsh (e.g., “not bioinspired enough”), viable fallbacks within the same 2026–2027 window are:
- **IEEE BHI / IEEE JBHI** (journal special issues on ophthalmic AI or trustworthy AI).
